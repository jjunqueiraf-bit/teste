# The Two Missions — grounded in real 2026 NASA programs

Both replace earlier concepts that failed a science check:
- ~~Moon: nitrogen energy production~~ → nitrogen is not an energy source
- ~~Mars: discover a new element~~ → periodic table complete to 118; new
  elements are made in accelerators, never found on planets

---

## MISSION A — MOON: Harvest Helium-3 to Power Earth

**This is the real version of "Moon energy for Earth."**

Lunar regolith holds helium-3, implanted over billions of years by solar wind.
Earth has almost none. He-3 is a candidate fusion fuel and is already in demand
today for quantum computing cryogenics and neutron detection.

### Real evidence (show the kids this)
- **May 2026** — NASA awarded **Interlune** a **$6.9M contract** for a payload
  to extract helium-3 and hydrogen from lunar soil
- Payload is called **Prospect Moon**, targeting a **2028** lunar launch
- A He-3 **prospecting camera** is due to fly on **Astrobotic's Griffin-1** lander
- **Interlune + Vermeer** built a working **excavator prototype**
- Harvesting and return to Earth targeted for the **2030s**

### Who pays (real sponsors)
| Type | Who |
|---|---|
| Government | NASA, US Dept of Energy, Department of the Air Force |
| Company | Interlune (ex-Blue Origin founders) |
| Industrial partner | Vermeer (excavation hardware) |
| Lander / delivery | Astrobotic (Griffin-1) |
| Commercial buyers | Bluefors, Maybell Quantum Industries |

### Honest caveat to teach
He-3 fusion is HARD — needs higher temperatures than conventional fusion, and
concentrations in regolith are tiny (parts per billion). Price estimates vary
wildly across sources ($9M/lb to $20M/kg). Good teaching moment: real science
has disagreement.

### Game trade-offs
Dig more regolith → more power and crew hours spent → less shielding, less O2.
Solar at the sunlit peaks vs water ice in permanently shadowed craters.

## MISSION B — MARS: Recover Sapphire Canyon

**This is the real version of "discover something new on Mars" — and it is
dramatically better than a fictional element, because the cliffhanger is REAL
and unresolved right now.**

### Real evidence
- Perseverance drilled **Sapphire Canyon** from the **Cheyava Falls** rock on
  **21 July 2024** (sol 1,215) — a 6.2 cm core, sample #25
- **Sept 2025** — NASA announced it as a **"potential biosignature"**, published
  in *Nature*. Strongest candidate for ancient life ever drilled
- The rock shows **"leopard spots"** — rings of iron and phosphate that on Earth
  form where microbes lived. Rich in organic carbon, sulfur, oxidized iron, phosphorus
- **NOT proof.** Geothermal heating could explain it. No cell, fossil, or
  biological polymer has been imaged
- **The sample is still sitting on Mars.** Mars Sample Return lost its funding in
  the FY2026 spending agreement signed **23 Jan 2026**
- Only **laboratory analysis on Earth** can settle whether it is life

### Who pays (real sponsors)
| Type | Who |
|---|---|
| Government | NASA, ESA (MSR partner before defunding) |
| Status | Mars Sample Return **defunded** Jan 2026 |
| Competitor | China is pursuing its own Mars sample return |

### Game trade-offs
Comms delay is REAL here (4–24 min) — send a request, wait, act without an answer.
Dust storms cut solar power. Every sol spent traversing costs life support.
