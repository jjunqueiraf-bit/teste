The complete game as a single offline HTML file.

**NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 - Sao Paulo**
**Challenge:** Space Mission Design Game
**Players:** 1-4, ages 7-12

Download `index.html` and double-click it. No install, no account, no server, no network - every image, animation and data record is embedded in the file.

### What it is
Players vote on a destination, then make the five decisions a real mission team makes: when to launch, where the power comes from, which two instruments to carry, how the evidence gets home, and which rocket can lift it. A design that breaks its mass, power, cost or launch-window limits cannot launch, and the game names the limit it broke. In flight, science only counts once it has been transmitted to Earth.

### NASA data used
- **DONKI** - 132 real coronal mass ejections (1 Oct - 14 Nov 2026), each storm using that event's real speed, half-angle and NASA class
- **NASA Image and Video Library** - ten photographs, each shown with its NASA ID
- **"Design and Develop Science Missions"** (NASA, 2025) - the six-step process the game is built around
- **CubeSat 101** - the mass and cost scales

### Use of AI
The team chose the challenge, designed both missions, decided every rule and drew the pixel art. The code was written with an AI assistant under the team's direction. The six crew portraits and the two orbital diagrams are AI-generated and carry visible watermarks saying so. Full declaration on the game's DATA, SOURCES & CREDITS screen.
